  💡 The Actual Answer

  The "SnarkGirl v. The World" multi-LLM debate arena IS a good feature — with important caveats the debate itself surfaced:

   1. Best for empirically-grounded technical topics — architectural decisions, security tradeoffs, dependency choices, performance strategies. Not ideal for
   pure opinion questions.
   2. Cross-model adversarial pressure is genuinely valuable — different AI models DO reason differently and catch each other's blind spots in ways
  self-red-teaming cannot replicate.
